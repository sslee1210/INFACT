Petrochemicals Company
We are expanding our customer-centric business through high-value-added specialty materials.
The Petrochemicals Company is LG Chem's major business area with world-class productivity and cost competitiveness. We have established a vertically integrated system from basic raw materials such as ethylene and propylene to downstream products such as PE, ABS, and synthetic rubber.
Advanced Materials Company
We aim to become a global top‑tier battery and electronic materials company leading next‑generation technologies.
The Advanced Materials Company is enhancing our business competitiveness in specialty materials through a market and customer-oriented approach.
Life Sciences Company
We aim to rise as a global pharmaceutical company that develops innovative new drugs.
As a new growth engine for the future to be fostered in the medium and long term, the Company has differentiated R&D capabilities that have received new drug approval from the U.S. FDA for the first time in Korea.
