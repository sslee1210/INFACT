Generic Medicines
We hold 310 registered products that improve quality of life and respond to the aging society, and we operate our own finished product production and consignment manufacturing business. Furthermore, we are exporting finished products of various formulations to Vietnam, Malaysia, Mongolia, Peru, and Turkey etc.
APIs
Based on accumulated technology in organic synthesis and fermentation for over 60 years period, our portfolio is comprised of more than 40 APIs focused on high value-added including ABK (Arbekacin sulfate) and SKD (Streptokinase,Streptodornase). We dedicate to offer high quality APIs to domestic and overseas customers.
Contrast Agents
In 2000, by introducing the original contrast agent OptiRay series, we entered the diagnostic/preventive medical field, and we achieved an average annual sales of KRW 30 billion with highly safe products, positioning ourselves as a leading company in the contrast agent field in Korea.
