Reimagining Drug-Delivery

with Microfluidic Engineering

Inventage Lab pioneers microfluidic-based drug-delivery platforms that enable exceptional particle uniformity, precise and tunable release kinetics, and seamless scalability from R&D to full commercial manufacturing. By powering long-acting injectables, high-concentration biologics, and next-generation nucleic-acid delivery, we redefine therapeutic performance and expand the boundaries of pharmaceutical innovation.

The Core Microfluidic Engine Behind IVL DDS Platforms:

Laminar-FLUIDigm™

Laminar-FLUIDigm™ is Inventage Lab’s proprietary microfluidic system engineered to produce ultra-uniform microspheres through stable laminar flow control. This technology ensures precise release kinetics, exceptional batch-to-batch reproducibility, and seamless scalability from early R&D to commercial manufacturing, enabling next-generation long-acting and nucleic-acid delivery solutions.

IVL-DrugFluidic®

A microfluidic long-acting injectable (LAI) platform that produces ultra-uniform microspheres for precise, tunable, and predictable drug release.

IVL-BioFluidic™

Ultra-concentrated biologics platform enabling stable, low-viscosity formulations optimized for subcutaneous injection.

IVL-GeneFluidic®

A microfluidic nucleic-acid delivery platform that enables controlled LNP manufacturing with high uniformity, scalability, and reproducibility.
